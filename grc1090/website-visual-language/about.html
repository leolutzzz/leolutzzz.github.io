Leo’s Chicken is all about serving crispy, flavorful fried chicken made fresh for every customer. Our goal is simple: bring people together over delicious comfort food that feels homemade.

From classic fried chicken and spicy wings to tender sandwiches and satisfying sides, every item is prepared with bold seasoning and quality ingredients. Whether you are grabbing a quick lunch or sharing a meal with friends, Leo’s Chicken has something for everyone.

We believe great food should come with great service. Our team works hard to create a welcoming place where customers can relax, enjoy their meal, and come back for their favorites.

At Leo’s Chicken, every bite is made to be crispy, juicy, and full of flavor. Thank you for supporting our restaurant—we cannot wait to serve you.
